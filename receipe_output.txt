### **Spiced Paneer Matar Paratha (Stuffed Indian Flatbread)**
This is a popular North Indian breakfast or lunch dish. Soft, crumbled paneer and sweet green peas are spiced and stuffed inside a wholesome whole-wheat flatbread, then roasted to golden perfection.

* **Prep time:** 20 minutes
* **Cook time:** 20 minutes
* **Servings:** 4 parathas
* **Diet:** Vegetarian

---

### **Ingredients**

#### **For the Dough:**
* 1 cup **Wheat flour** (Atta)
* 1 tsp Oil or Ghee
* A pinch of Salt
* Water (approx. ½ cup, to knead)

#### **For the Paneer-Matar Stuffing:**
* ½ cup **Paneer** (grated or finely crumbled)
* ½ cup **Matar** (Green peas - fresh or frozen)
* 1 Green chili, finely chopped
* ½ inch Ginger, finely grated
* ¼ tsp Cumin seeds (jeera)
* ¼ tsp Turmeric powder
* ½ tsp Red chili powder (adjust to taste)
* ½ tsp Garam masala
* ½ tsp Amchur powder (dry mango powder) or lemon juice
* Salt to taste
* 2 tbsp Coriander leaves, finely chopped
* 1 tsp Oil (for cooking the peas)

#### **For Roasting:**
* 2–3 tbsp Ghee or Oil 

---

### **Instructions**

#### **Step 1: Prepare the Dough**
1. In a large mixing bowl, add the **wheat flour**, a pinch of salt, and 1 teaspoon of oil. 
2. Gradually add water and knead into a smooth, soft, and pliable dough (similar to roti dough).
3. Cover the dough with a damp cloth and let it rest for 15 minutes.

#### **Step 2: Prepare the Paneer-Matar Stuffing**
1. **Cook the Peas:** If using fresh peas, boil them until soft. If using frozen, thaw them. Coarsely mash the green peas using a potato masher or grind them briefly in a blender (do not make a smooth paste; keep it textured).
2. **Sauté the aromatics:** Heat 1 teaspoon of oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the cumin seeds and let them splutter. Add the chopped green chili and grated ginger. Sauté for 30 seconds.
3. **Cook the filling:** Add the mashed peas, turmeric, red chili powder, garam masala, amchur powder, and salt. Cook for 2 minutes to remove excess moisture.
4. **Mix in Paneer:** Turn off the heat. Add the crumbled **paneer** and freshly chopped coriander leaves. Mix everything thoroughly. Let the stuffing cool completely.

#### **Step 3: Assemble and Roll the Parathas**
1. Divide the rested dough into 4 equal-sized balls. Divide the paneer-matar stuffing into 4 equal portions.
2. Take one dough ball, dust it with dry wheat flour, and roll it into a 4-inch circle.
3. Place one portion of the stuffing in the center. 
4. Bring all the edges of the dough together in the center, pinch them to seal, and press the joint down gently.
5. Dust the stuffed ball with dry wheat flour. Roll it out gently into a 6 to 7-inch circle, applying even pressure so the stuffing doesn't leak out.

#### **Step 4: Roast the Parathas**
1. Heat a tawa (griddle) over medium-high heat. 
2. Place the rolled paratha on the hot tawa. Cook for a minute until small bubbles start to appear.
3. Flip the paratha, drizzle ½ teaspoon of ghee or oil on the cooked side, and spread it evenly.
4. Flip again and apply ghee/oil to the other side. 
5. Press down gently with a spatula and cook until both sides are golden-brown and crispy.

---

### **Serving Suggestion**
Serve the hot **Paneer Matar Parathas** straight off the griddle with a dollop of butter on top. Pair them with fresh plain yogurt (curd), a spicy Indian pickle, or green coriander chutney.
